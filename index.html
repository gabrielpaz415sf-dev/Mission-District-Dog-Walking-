About Gabriel

I'm Gabriel Paz, and I've been walking dogs around Bryant Street and the surrounding Mission blocks for years, from the sunny sidewalks along 24th to the loops around Garfield Square. Clients count on me to show up on time, every time, and to send a quick note and photo after each walk. I keep groups small so every dog gets real attention, and I get to know each one's pace, quirks, and favorite corners. If you're looking for someone dependable who genuinely enjoys the work, I'd love to meet you and your dog.

— Gabriel
